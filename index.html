While you wait: first steps

1. Stay safeMove away from danger if you can. Don’t put yourself at risk.
2. Call and stay on the lineGive your location, what happened and how many people are hurt. Follow the dispatcher’s instructions.
3. Give basic careCheck breathing. Press firmly on heavy bleeding with clean cloth. Start CPR only if trained and the person isn’t breathing.
4. Stay with themKeep the person calm and still until help arrives. Unlock the door and turn on lights so responders can find you.
